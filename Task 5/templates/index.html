Machine Learning & NLP Architecture

01

Corpus Ingestion & Preprocessing

Ingests 550 tech job postings and 160 intern profiles. Preserves compound technical terms (scikit-learn, c++, ci/cd, deep_learning), strips non-informative stopwords, and performs technical tokenization.

02

TF-IDF Feature Space

Extracts unigrams and bigrams using sublinear term-frequency scaling. Generates high-dimensional sparse representations capturing domain-specific technical vocabulary.

03

K-Means Clustering (k=6)

Clusters industry job postings into natural domain groupings. Computes cluster centroids and extracts top characteristic TF-IDF keywords per cluster.

04

Skill Gap & Cosine Similarity

Transforms intern profiles into the TF-IDF space and calculates vector Cosine Similarity against domain centroids. Evaluates exact skill coverage ratios to compute an overall Readiness Score.

05

Upskilling Recommendation

Automated mapping of missing critical skills to specialized coursework, practical capstone projects, and estimated completion timelines.
